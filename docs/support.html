Support

Write to ellipsio.studio@gmail.com. A person at Ellipsio Studio reads every message, usually within a few business days.

Setting up for the first time? Follow the installation guide.

What to include

  • What you tried to do and what happened instead.
  • The exact error message, copied as text.
  • Your operating system and the AutoUpload version (in the app under About, or from autoupload --version).

Never send your token files, your client_secret.json or your Google password. Nobody at Ellipsio Studio will ever ask for them.

Privacy requests

For access, rectification, cancellation or opposition requests (ARCO rights under Peru's Law No. 29733) or any other privacy question, write to the same address with the subject “Privacy request”. The Privacy Policy explains your rights.

Common questions

How do I disconnect AutoUpload from my channel?

Remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions, press Disconnect in the app, or run autoupload auth <channel> logout. Then delete %APPDATA%\AutoUpload (~/.config/autoupload on macOS and Linux) if you want no local trace left.

Google says “Access blocked: … has not completed the Google verification process”.

The app is in Testing and the Google account you signed in with is not a test user. In Google Cloud Console, open Google Auth Platform → Audience → Test users, add that account’s email address and sign in again. See installation guide, §6.

Why do I have to sign in again every week?

While an app is in Testing, Google expires every authorization seven days after consent. Press Reconnect in the app (or run autoupload auth <channel> login) again, or select Publish app in Audience so the access no longer expires. A Personal app never expires this way. See installation guide, §4.

Google says “Google hasn’t verified this app”.

This is expected for a published app that Google has not verified yet. If the app and its OAuth client are your own, choose Advanced → Go to … to continue. Unverified apps are limited to 100 users.

An upload fails saying the YouTube Data API is not enabled.

Enable YouTube Data API v3 in APIs & Services → Library of the same Google Cloud project as your OAuth client, wait a few minutes and try again. See installation guide, §8.

Does my app need Google verification?

Not while it is in Personal mode or in Testing: Google requires no verification in either. Each installation is its own app in its owner’s Google Cloud project, so one person’s setup never depends on another’s. Verification only matters if you publish your project for people outside your test users. Then Google asks for brand verification (a home page that describes the app, on a domain you have verified in Google Search Console, with the same privacy policy linked from the home page and the consent screen) and, because the YouTube scopes are sensitive, a demonstration video of the sign-in and the upload plus a reason for each scope. YouTube scopes are not restricted, so no paid security assessment applies. See Google’s verification requirements.

Why is there no “Sign in with Google” button on this site?

Because there is nothing on a server to sign in to. AutoUpload runs on your computer, and your access token never leaves it. A shared sign-in would mean every user authorizing one Ellipsio Studio project, which Google would first have to verify, and all users sharing that project’s daily quota of 100 uploads. With your own client, the quota and the access are yours alone, and you depend on no one else’s project.

Does AutoUpload see my Google password?

No. You sign in on Google's own page. AutoUpload only receives the access token Google issues for the permissions you allowed.

Does Ellipsio Studio see my videos or my channel?

No. There is no AutoUpload server. Your files go from your computer straight to YouTube.

Why did an upload not go out?

The most common causes are the YouTube API's daily quota, an expired or revoked token, or a file YouTube rejected. Check the message AutoUpload printed and your channel in YouTube Studio, then write to us with the details above.

Does it cost anything?

No. AutoUpload is free and sells nothing.
