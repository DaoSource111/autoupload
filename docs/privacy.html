Privacy Policy

AutoUpload runs on your computer and talks only to Google. Ellipsio Studio does not collect, receive or store your data. This page explains exactly what the app touches and how to take it back.

§1Who is responsible

AutoUpload is made available by Ellipsio Studio, an independent studio based in Peru, contact ellipsio.studio@gmail.com.

AutoUpload is software you run yourself. Your data is processed on your own device, under your control, and is sent only to Google. Ellipsio Studio has no server, no account system and no copy of your data.

§2Google user data accessed

When you connect a YouTube channel, Google shows its consent screen and you grant AutoUpload these OAuth scopes, and only these. Each line below is Google's own wording; select it to see how AutoUpload uses that permission.

“Manage your YouTube videos”https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
What AutoUpload does with it
Uploads the video files you queued, as private videos with a scheduled publish time, and sets their thumbnails.
What it never does with it
It never uploads a file you did not queue, and it never uploads to a channel other than the one you connected.
“See, edit, and permanently delete your YouTube videos, ratings, comments and captions”https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl
What AutoUpload does with it
Adds the caption files you supplied, adds the new video to the playlist you named, and updates the videos it uploaded itself (for example, their scheduled publish time).
What it never does with it
  • It never deletes a video.
  • It never rates videos, and never reads, posts or deletes comments.
  • It never edits videos it did not upload.
Why Google's wording is broader
Google groups captions, playlists and video updates under this single permission; there is no narrower one. The sentence describes everything the permission would allow, not what AutoUpload does.
“View your YouTube account”https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly
What AutoUpload does with it
Reads your channel's ID, to confirm it is uploading to the channel you connected, and the dates of your scheduled and published videos, to pick the next free time slot.
What it never does with it
It never reads your watch history, subscriptions, messages or analytics, and it stores nothing it reads except the channel ID.

Those three are the only permissions AutoUpload requests. It never asks for your email address, your name or profile, Gmail, Drive, contacts or any Google Cloud service, and it never sees your Google password.

You sign in on Google's own page; AutoUpload never sees or stores your Google password.

§3How the data is used

  • Only to perform the uploads and scheduling you ask for, on the channel you authorized.
  • Never for advertising, never sold, never shared with data brokers, and never used to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • No person at Ellipsio Studio reads your data, because it never reaches Ellipsio Studio.

AutoUpload's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

§4Where it is stored and how it is protected

Everything AutoUpload keeps stays on your computer. There is no external database and no AutoUpload server.

ItemWhere it livesContents
Access token%APPDATA%\AutoUploadThe OAuth token Google issues after you allow access. It lets the app act on your channel without asking you to sign in each time.
Upload logstate.sqlite in your channel folderFile name, YouTube video ID and scheduled time of each upload, so no file is uploaded twice.
Videos and detailsFolders you chooseYour own files. AutoUpload reads them and sends them to YouTube; it does not copy them elsewhere.
  • The token sits in your user profile, which Windows protects with your account. Turning on disk encryption (BitLocker or similar) adds a further layer.
  • All traffic between AutoUpload and Google is encrypted with HTTPS (TLS).
  • The app's OAuth client secret is never published, on this site or in any code repository.
  • AutoUpload contains no analytics, telemetry or crash reporting.

§5Sharing and international transfer

The only recipient of your data is Google LLC, in the United States, which runs the YouTube Data API. Sending your videos and their details there is the purpose of the app, so using it involves a cross-border transfer of that data from your country to the United States. Google handles it under the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service.

AutoUpload shares nothing with Ellipsio Studio or with any other third party.

§6How long it is kept

  • Access token: until you revoke access or delete it. Google may also expire it; while an OAuth app is in Testing, Google expires it seven days after consent.
  • Upload log: until you delete the file.
  • Your videos on YouTube: kept by YouTube under its own terms until you delete them there.

§7Your consent

AutoUpload works only after you give explicit consent: you start the connection yourself, sign in on Google's page and choose Allow on Google's consent screen, which lists the permissions above. Nothing is uploaded before that. You can withdraw your consent at any time, as described next.

§8Revoking access and deleting data

  1. Open myaccount.google.com/permissions, choose AutoUpload and remove its access. Or run autoupload auth <channel> logout, which revokes the token with Google and deletes it.
  2. Delete the folder %APPDATA%\AutoUpload and the state.sqlite file in your channel folder.

After these two steps, nothing AutoUpload stored remains.

§9Your rights

Peru (Law No. 29733 on Personal Data Protection). You have the rights of access, rectification, cancellation and opposition (ARCO), and the right to information. Because AutoUpload keeps your data only on your device, you can exercise most of them yourself with the steps in §8. For any request or question, write to ellipsio.studio@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request”; we answer within the deadlines set by the law's regulations. If you are not satisfied, you may file a claim with the Peruvian National Authority for Personal Data Protection (Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales, Ministry of Justice and Human Rights).

Ellipsio Studio does not create or hold a personal data bank with AutoUpload users' data, so there is no data bank to register with the National Registry for Personal Data Protection.

European Economic Area and United Kingdom (GDPR). You have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and you may complain to your data protection authority. The legal basis for the processing on your device is your consent and your own request to upload.

California (CCPA/CPRA). Ellipsio Studio does not sell or share personal information and does not collect it.

§10This website

  • This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads no fonts, scripts or images from third parties: its typeface is served from the site itself.
  • If you switch language or between night and light view, your browser's localStorage keeps the key au-lang (en or es) or au-theme (dark or light). They stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. You can clear them from your browser settings.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

§11Children

AutoUpload is not directed at children under 13 and follows YouTube's own age requirements for account holders.

§12Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it. If AutoUpload ever needed new permissions, Google would ask for your consent again.
