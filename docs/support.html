Support

Write to ellipsio.studio@gmail.com. A person at Ellipsio Studio reads every message, usually within a few business days.

Setting up for the first time? Follow the installation guide.

What to include

  • What you tried to do and what happened instead.
  • The exact error message, copied as text.
  • Your Windows and Python versions.

Never send your token files, your client_secret.json or your Google password. Nobody at Ellipsio Studio will ever ask for them.

Privacy requests

For access, rectification, cancellation or opposition requests (ARCO rights under Peru's Law No. 29733) or any other privacy question, write to the same address with the subject “Privacy request”. The Privacy Policy explains your rights.

Common questions

How do I disconnect AutoUpload from my channel?

Remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or run subidor auth <channel> logout. Then delete %APPDATA%\subidor-youtube if you want no local trace left.

Does AutoUpload see my Google password?

No. You sign in on Google's own page. AutoUpload only receives the access token Google issues for the permissions you allowed.

Does Ellipsio Studio see my videos or my channel?

No. There is no AutoUpload server. Your files go from your computer straight to YouTube.

Why did an upload not go out?

The most common causes are the YouTube API's daily quota, an expired or revoked token, or a file YouTube rejected. Check the message AutoUpload printed and your channel in YouTube Studio, then write to us with the details above.

Does it cost anything?

No. AutoUpload is free and sells nothing.
