# Code of Conduct

AutoUpload is a small open source project. We want it to be a welcoming place for everyone who uses it,
reports problems, or helps build it.

## Our expectations

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to Python, Git or YouTube's API.
- Assume good intent, and say clearly when something is a problem.
- Keep feedback about the work, not about the person.
- Respect privacy: do not post channel IDs, tokens, client files or personal data from anyone's account.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.

## Handling problems

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues or pull requests, and block people who repeatedly
break these expectations. Serious cases may be reported by email.

## Contact

Report concerns to ellipsio.studio@gmail.com. Reports are read by the maintainers and kept confidential.
